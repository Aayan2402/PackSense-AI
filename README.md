## 📌 About the Project

**PackSense AI** is an AI-based intelligent food packaging recommendation system designed to help farmers, food producers, FPOs, and small businesses select suitable packaging materials for different food commodities.

The system considers food type, storage conditions, target shelf life, transportation routes, climate, packaging cost, and sustainability to provide suitable packaging recommendations.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Selecting the right packaging material is important for maintaining food quality, extending shelf life, reducing food wastage, and controlling packaging costs. PackSense AI aims to simplify this process through intelligent recommendations.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **AI Recommendation Engine:** Suggests suitable packaging materials based on food requirements.
- **Precision Barrier Engine:** Evaluates Oxygen Transmission Rate (OTR) and Water Vapor Transmission Rate (WVTR).
- **AI Visual PackAudit:** Supports packaging image analysis.
- **Route & Climate Intelligence:** Considers transportation routes and climate conditions.
- **Farmer & SME Mode:** Provides simple and accessible recommendations.
- **Industry & Lab Mode:** Provides detailed packaging information.
- **Green & Cost Scorecard:** Compares estimated packaging cost and sustainability.
- **Material Comparison:** Helps users compare different packaging options.
- **Multilingual Support:** English, Hindi, and Marathi.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Select the food commodity.
2. Enter its condition and target shelf life.
3. Provide storage and transportation details.
4. Enter packaging and environmental requirements.
5. The recommendation engine evaluates the inputs.
6. View suggested packaging materials and their properties, estimated cost, and sustainability score.
